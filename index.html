Everything the host needs. Fair games every player enjoys.

Vexel handles the rotation; you just tap who won.

Equal games for everyone

Whoever is furthest behind their fair share plays next. Late arrivals get their share for the time they're there, and nobody sits out twice in a row when it can be avoided.

Balanced teams

Skill levels A to E keep matches close. Don't know someone's level? Vexel learns it from who wins.

New partners, every game

Vexel avoids putting the same teammates together again whenever it can, so everyone mixes.

Singles, doubles, any format

Timed games or play to a score. Doubles, singles or both, with singles offered automatically when a few players would otherwise wait.

TV display for players

Plug in a TV or projector: everyone sees the courts, who's next and the time left, without crowding the host.

Keeps working if the internet drops

Open Vexel once and it keeps running even if the Wi-Fi gets cut mid-session. The Windows app goes further and runs fully offline from the start. No account needed. Everything is saved as you go, every action can be undone, and you can export a spreadsheet to finish on paper anytime.

DUPR-ready for pickleball

Add players' DUPR IDs once, record full scores, and export a file ready for DUPR's club upload (Import Matches via CSV). Side-out or rally scoring. Short-handed and unscored games are left out automatically.

Vexel Web works in any modern browser on a Mac, iPad, iPhone, Android, Chromebook or PC. Open it once with internet and it keeps working even if the Wi-Fi or internet gets cut. For the best experience, add it to your Dock or home screen (Safari: Share → Add to Dock / Add to Home Screen; Chrome: Install app). Your sessions stay in that browser on that device, so download a backup from Settings now and then.

Vexel for Windows

Want the full offline experience on Windows? Install the app. It runs entirely on your laptop with no internet needed. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), updates install automatically when you're online.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
